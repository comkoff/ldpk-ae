If any trademark holder considers this usage unclear or inappropriate, we kindly invite them to contact us. 2The LDPK The Lens Distortion Plugin Kit (LDPK) is a collection of C++-sources and -headers, GLSL-sources, python scripts and shell scripts. It has been introduced in 2011, primarily in order to support the development of lens distortion plugins for &tde4;. Later, the LDPK became the starting point for all topics related to lens distortion, e.g. interoperability between applications used in the postproduction pipeline. Roughly speaking, the LDPK contains the following.
  • Ready-to-use Plugins and scripts for &nuke; versions ≥ &nuke_earliest_version;.
  • Shaders, GUI-specification files and scripts for &flame; matchbox nodes
  • Headers for implementing your own &tde4; lens distortion plugins
  • Python bindings and wrapper for &tde4;'s lens distortion models
  • Documentation on the Standard Models of &tde4;
  • Documentation on the Fisheye Models of &tde4; (experimental, shipped with the LDPK as plugins)
  • A tool for creating a standard-UV-map, &openexr;, with distinct Data and Display Window.
2.1The lens distortion models The current version of the LDPK supports more lens distortion models than are provided by &tde4;. The most important models are the following:
  • 3DE4 Anamorphic - Standard, Degree 4 – This is the standard model for anamorphic lenses, including parameters for lens rotation (for manually mounted anamorphots). It is based on a polynomial correction factor of order four.
  • 3DE4 Anamorphic - Standard, Degree 6 – Similar to Standard, Degree 4 but with a polynomial correction factor of order six.
  • 3DE4 Anamorphic - Rescaled, Degree 4 – Similar to Standard, Degree 4 but intended for non-uniformly rescaled images.
  • 3DE4 Anamorphic - Rescaled, Degree 6 – Similar to Standard, Degree 6 but intended for non-uniformly rescaled images.
  • 3DE4 Radial - Standard, Degree 4 – This is the standard model for radial symmetric lenses with correction terms for lens decentering, based on the original models by Brown (1966) and Conrady (1919). In contrast to the original model we have re-mapped the decentering parameters so that the model function is linear in these parameters. The price of doing so is one additional non-physical parameter of degree four.
As of &tde4r8; there are two additional lens distortion models which are able to compensate artefacts from camera internal stabilizing. Since the stabilizer parameters are often adjusted per-frame, a good amount of survey is required in order to use these models (see featurette video 3DE4 R8.0: New features). The stabilizer models are:
  • 3DE4 Anamorphic - Stabilizer, Degree 6 – Like Rescaled, Degree 6 but with two additional stabilizer parameters.
  • 3DE4 Radial - Stabilizer, Degree 6 – A degree-6 model with degree-2 decentering (like Brown-Conrady, Degree 6,2) and two stabilizer parameters.
In addition to these models &tde4; provides the following deprecated model:
  • LD 3DE Classic LD Model – This models is essentially a degree four model polynomial for radial lenses, attempting to incorporate anamorphic aspects through a single squeeze-parameter.
The current fisheye model of &tde4; is
  • LD 3DE Radial - Fisheye, Degree 8
Although it has its limitations, it is the only fisheye model fully supported in &tde4;. There are four more fisheye models with different mapping functions. While these models are not directly implemented in &tde4; they are available as plugins on &linux; and &macos; and also work with the network calculation service &tde4mhp;. Fisheye lenses often have well-defined mapping functions, which are named Equidistant, Equisolid Angle, Orthographic, and Stereographic in the literature. By choosing the right model for a given lens, distortion parameters should remain low, making the handling easier.
  • LD 3DE4 Radial Fisheye Equidistant, Degree 8
  • LD 3DE4 Radial Fisheye Equisolid, Degree 8
  • LD 3DE4 Radial Fisheye Orthographic, Degree 8
  • LD 3DE4 Radial Fisheye Stereographic, Degree 8
The model 3DE4 Anamorphic Degree 6 has been completely removed in &tde4r7_1; and is no longer part of the LDPK. Its functionality is now covered by model 3DE4 Anamorphic - Standard, Degree_6.

There are two toy models
  • 3DE4 Radial - Homomorphic, Degree 2
  • 3DE4 Anamorphic - Homomorphic, Degree_2
These models prioritize a low number of parameters. However, it is highly likely that they are oversimplified and not suitable for production use.

Finally, we have included the model
  • 3DE4 Brown-Conrady, Degree 6,2
as plugins for &linux; and &macos;, as it is utilized in at least one widely adopted game engine. This implementation follows the Brown-Conrady model, featuring a degree six for the radial part and a degree of two for decentering. 3Exporting lens distortion data 3.1Lens distortion nodes for &nuke; 3.1.1Installing ready-to-use nodes There is a compiled plugin for each combination of lens distortion model, operating system and &nuke; version.
