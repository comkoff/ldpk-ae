bool initializeParameters ()
 Implies setting the rho-factor for mapping between dn- and OpenCV-coordinates. rho is based on the following: The OpenCV literature says, the model is based on filmback coordinates, which means the coefficients are not dimensionless. Our dn-coordinates are expressed in filmback coordinates like x_cm = x_dn * r_fb_cm and y_cm = y_dn * r_fb_cm. Our cm-coordinates describe a virtual filmback, where x_cm = x_cm_phys * r_pa and y_cm = y_cm_phys. In OpenCV it seems that virtual and physical Filmback are defined as x_cm = x_cm_phys and y_cm = y_cm_phys / r_pa. Their model is based on their virtual filmback, so that x_cm_ocv = x_cm_sdv / r_pa = x_dn * r_fb_cm / r_pa and y_cm_ocv = y_cm_sdv / r_pa = y_dn * r_fb_cm / r_pa. We define rho by x_cm_ocv = rho * x_dn, and hence rho = r_fb_cm / r_pa.
